|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paper PDF (`paper/factmm_rag.pdf`) not yet available; arXiv fetch blocked by sandbox network policy | Source material | High | Certain (currently true) | Ask user to upload PDF or paste text; keep all paper-sourced claims labeled `UNKNOWN` until then; do not fabricate | Open |

| 10 | Committing large files (weights, embeddings, datasets) accidentally | Repo hygiene | High | Low (if `.gitignore` maintained) | Existing `.gitignore` already excludes datasets, checkpoints, logs, env files; re-verify before every commit involving new binary-ish paths | Mitigated |
